LOCKOUT TAGOUT TRYOUT (LOTOTO)

A Study Guide on Zero Energy Verification

Introduction

Parking lots and job sites present invisible electrical hazards. At Chargie, our field operations involve deploying high-voltage infrastructure where standard safety isn't enough. Lockout Tagout Tryout (LOTOTO) takes basic protection to a critical final stage. It isn’t just about applying a lock; it is about verifying absolute isolation before a single tool touches a live circuit.

"In the presence of operational hazards, absolute vigilance is the only guarantee."

Why It Matters

  • EV infrastructure operates on multi-ton electrical currents.
  • Unexpected energization during service causes catastrophic, fatal injuries.
  • Warning tags alone do not physically stop a circuit from active conduction.
  • The "Tryout" step guarantees the machine cannot operate before work begins.

When You Are At Risk

Main Switchgear Work

Interfacing with commercial panels and electrical rooms where distribution paths carry continuous live load.

Pedestal Maintenance

Servicing charger internals where residual power or capacitor blocks hold stored hazards post-shutdown.

Shared Contractor Sites

Active deployment locations where secondary trades can accidentally toggle or clear a breaker connection.

Complacency Breaks

Rushing to meet standard delivery metrics and clean parameters while bypassing the final verification loop.

The Cost of a Slip

  • • Severe arc flash explosions and fatal electrocution vectors.
  • • Irreparable infrastructure property damage to commercial panel networks.
  • • Immediate OSHA field inspections, heavy corporate penalties, and site shutdown.

Small mistake. Big impact.

Field Checklist

Verify operations prior to site clearance

  • ✓ All on-site personnel explicitly notified of the electrical distribution isolation.
  • ✓ Personal safety padlock firmly secured to the core breaker plate or lock box.
  • ✓ Warning tag filled with name, validation date, and deployment team signatures.
  • ✓ Tryout confirmation run executed by attempting to cycle standard panel power keys.
