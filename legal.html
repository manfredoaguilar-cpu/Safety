SMS Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 15, 2024

At Chargie LLC, we are committed to protecting the privacy of our employees and contractors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information specifically related to our SMS Safety Training and Compliance Campaign.

1. Information Collection

We collect your mobile phone number directly from you during the onboarding process. This information is used exclusively for internal operational communications, specifically safety training modules and compliance reminders.

2. Use of Information

  • Send automated links to safety training modules.
  • Send reminders for outstanding compliance tasks.
  • Notify you of critical safety updates relevant to your role.

3. Data Sharing

Chargie LLC does not sell, rent, or lease your personal information or mobile phone numbers to third parties. Phone numbers provided for SMS purposes will not be shared with third-party affiliates for marketing purposes.

4. Opt-Out Rights

You may opt-out of SMS communications at any time by replying "STOP" to any message received. Please note that opting out of safety communications may impact your ability to fulfill compliance requirements.
